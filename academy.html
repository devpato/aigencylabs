One working skill.

04 Questions

Things everyone
asks first.

Do participants need a technical background?
No. The Academy is explicitly designed for non-technical staff. We've trained marketers, lawyers, accountants, recruiters, customer-success leads, and a chef. If you can describe your own job clearly, you'll be fine.
What tools and platforms do you teach?
We're platform-agnostic and teach the underlying patterns first. Where it matters, we work with Claude (our default), ChatGPT, and the AI features inside your existing stack, whatever your team is actually using.
What does a participant actually walk away with?
At minimum, one shipped, working skill they use in their real job, plus the vocabulary and judgment to design more. Teams typically walk away with 6–12 reusable internal skills by the end of a cohort.
Can you deliver this in Spanish?
Yes. Cohorts are delivered in English or Spanish depending on the audience. Mixed-language cohorts use English as the working language with bilingual instructors on hand.
What's the time commitment per participant?
Roughly 1–2 hours of live sessions per week, plus a little optional async practice. Designed to be absorbed alongside a normal working week, not on top of it.
